# Brand

This guideline records {{name}}'s brand decisions and how to apply them: the name, the voice, and the visual identity. The files that carry the brand, such as logos, icons, and fonts, are cataloged where the project keeps its assets.

## Name

<!--
- How the name is written in prose (capitalization, spacing), and where a system requires another form (domains, package scopes, handles, file names, bundle identifiers).
- How it is pronounced, if that is not obvious.
- What it means: the story the name tells, which may differ from how it was coined.
- Why it was kept: what was checked (pronunciation across languages, collisions with other products, search noise, domains), on what date, and its known weaknesses, accepted with it. Nothing here is legal clearance; say so if no trademark search was done.
- Rejected alternatives, as a table of name, meaning, and why it lost.
- If the project names things under the brand (packages, features, plans), the rule for when something carries the brand name and when it gets a neutral one, and the vocabulary for neutral names.
-->

This section refines it for {{name}}: where the two differ on tone, this section wins for this project, and the guardrails hold either way. Leave it empty to use the default unchanged.

## Visual identity

<!--
The idea: the one thing the identity comes back to, and why the mark, colors, and motion follow from it.
-->

### Logo

<!--
- The mark and the full logo, and which version to use where: color and one-color, dark and light artwork, the app icon and favicon. Say what "dark" and "light" mean in file names.
- Construction: geometry, typeface and tracking of a wordmark, and the optical adjustments, precise enough to redraw it.
- Clear space and minimum sizes, on screen and in print.
- Misuse: what not to do, such as retyping the wordmark, recoloring, stretching, or placing it where it loses contrast.
- Animation, if any: what moves, timing, easing, and what is shown when reduced motion is requested.
- Rejected marks and why each lost.
-->

### Color

<!--
- The palette as tokens, with the source format (for example OKLCH) and derived values (hex, RGB, print starting points), and the role of each.
- Approved foreground and background pairings with their contrast ratios, and what each is approved for.
- Proportion: how much of a layout each color may cover.
- Which look is primary (dark or light), and how it relates to the interface's theme setting.
- Why these values, and the palettes that were rejected.
-->

### Typography

<!--
- Each typeface with its role, license, and fallback.
- A type scale, if the brand sets one.
- Why each typeface was chosen, and the alternatives rejected.
-->

### Iconography and graphic elements

<!--
- The icon family, its license, and how icons are sized and colored next to text.
- Graphic devices drawn from the identity, with rules for using them.
-->

### Motion

<!--
- Durations, easing, and the one moment of play, if there is one.
- How every animation honors reduced motion.
-->

### Print and merchandise

<!--
- Export format, color conversion, and proofing notes for printers.
- Which logo version to use for each process (one ink, embroidery, full color).
-->
